# AI Procurement & P2P Automation

### AI-Assisted Procurement, Procure-to-Pay & ERP Workflow Automation

A professional portfolio project by **Faysal Ahmed (FaysalAhmedAIERP)** focused on combining procurement expertise, ERP workflows, Python automation, SOP-driven controls and AI-assisted decision support.

---

## Project Overview

This project explores how Artificial Intelligence and workflow automation can support end-to-end Procurement and Procure-to-Pay (P2P) activities while maintaining organizational controls and human accountability.

The project focuses on transforming repetitive procurement activities into structured, traceable and automation-ready workflows.

The core principle is:

> **AI recommends and explains; authorized humans approve; ERP executes approved procurement actions.**

## Proposed Architecture

### 1. Procurement Data Layer

Potential inputs may include:

- Purchase requisitions
- Vendor master data
- Supplier quotations
- Purchase orders
- Proforma invoices
- Contracts
- Goods receipt records
- Supplier invoices
- Payment documentation
- Procurement history
- Inventory information

### 2. SOP & Business Rules Layer

Procurement procedures can be converted into structured business rules.

Examples:

- Approval thresholds
- Minimum quotation requirements
- Vendor-selection criteria
- Budget validation
- Documentation requirements
- Purchase-order controls
- Invoice-matching rules
- Supplier compliance requirements

### 3. AI Assistance Layer

AI can support procurement users through:

- Quotation summarization
- Comparative analysis
- Supplier comparison
- Document review
- Exception identification
- Procurement recommendations
- Workflow guidance
- Draft communication
- Data extraction
- Decision-support explanations

### 4. Human Approval Layer

Authorized personnel remain responsible for procurement decisions such as:

- Supplier selection
- Commercial approval
- Purchase approval
- Contract acceptance
- Exception approval
- Payment authorization

### 5. ERP Execution Layer

Approved transactions can then be processed through ERP or enterprise systems.

## Human-in-the-Loop Procurement Model

This project follows a controlled human-in-the-loop approach.

- AI assists procurement users rather than replacing accountable decision-makers.
- Supplier-selection decisions remain subject to authorized human review.
- Purchase approvals remain controlled by organizational authority.
- AI recommendations should be explainable and traceable.
- Procurement activities should follow approved SOPs and policies.
- Transactions should maintain appropriate audit trails.
- Sensitive commercial information should be protected.

---

## Governance Principles

### Accountability

Authorized personnel remain responsible for procurement decisions.

### Transparency

AI recommendations should provide understandable supporting information.

### Traceability

Important recommendations, approvals and transactions should maintain audit records.

### Segregation of Duties

Requesting, approving, receiving and payment activities should follow appropriate organizational controls.

### Data Protection

Vendor, pricing, commercial and financial information should be appropriately protected.

### Controlled Automation

AI should not independently execute high-impact procurement transactions without authorization.
